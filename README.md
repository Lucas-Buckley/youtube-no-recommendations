# YouTube No Recommendations

A Chrome extension that keeps video links visible in **Subscriptions**, **channel pages**, and **search results**. It hides the Home feed, watch-page recommendations, player suggestions, and video links on other YouTube pages, including History and playlists. You can still open a video directly and use its player.

Shorts stay on the opened clip when you scroll, swipe, or use the navigation keys. On watch pages, the extension switches YouTube Autoplay off and disables the next-video shortcut. A video opened with a playlist link plays on its own.

## Install in Chrome, Opera, or Opera GX

1. Extract the ZIP if you downloaded it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome or `opera:extensions` in Opera or Opera GX.
3. Turn on **Developer mode**, then click **Load unpacked**.
4. Choose the `youtube-no-recommendations` folder containing `manifest.json`.
5. Reload any YouTube tabs that were already open.

If you already installed an older version, replace the files in the previously loaded folder, click **Reload** on its card on your browser’s extensions page, and refresh your YouTube tabs.

To disable the extension, use the switch on its card on the extensions page and reload YouTube. If you want YouTube to advance automatically again, turn Autoplay on in the player.
